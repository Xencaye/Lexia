LEXIA @LEXIA_VERSION@ - reads text on your screen out loud
================================================

Lexia reads text aloud from anywhere on the screen: web pages, images, videos and
games (including full-screen games). It works offline - nothing is sent anywhere,
unless you ask Lexia to download a voice.

HOW TO START
  1. Unzip this folder anywhere you like (for example on the Desktop).
     (Or use the installer, Lexia-@LEXIA_VERSION@-setup.exe, if you have it.)
  2. Double-click lexia.exe. The Lexia window opens. You do not need to be an administrator.
  3. Lexia is Off the first time. Press the big power button on the home page to turn it On.
  4. Turn your speakers on.

  Lexia lives in the tray, next to the clock. Closing its window does not end Lexia.
  Right-click the tray icon to turn Lexia On or Off, to open its window, or to quit.

HOW TO USE (default keys)
  F8   Freeze the screen and open the overlay.
         - Hover over text to see what would be read.
         - Click on text to read that whole paragraph.
         - Click and drag to read an area.
       You can pick as many things as you like. They are read one after another.
       Press F8 again, Esc, or right-click to leave the overlay. Reading goes on.
  F7   Pause or resume reading (only while Lexia is speaking or paused).
  F6   Tap to skip to the next paragraph; tap three times quickly to stop reading
       (only while Lexia is speaking or paused).

  The overlay appears on the monitor you were last working on. Its control bar has
  Pause, Skip, Stop and Help buttons.

SETTINGS
  Open the Lexia window (double-click the tray icon). There you can:
    - change the keys, the voice, its speed, pitch and volume
    - download or add voices
    - turn on read-along (a bar under the word you hear), dark mode, left-handed
      mouse, run at startup, and run as administrator (for games that run as
      administrator)
  Lexia keeps its settings in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Lexia (type that into the Explorer
  address bar). If a settings.json sits next to lexia.exe and none is there, Lexia
  uses that one.

GOOD TO KNOW
  - While Lexia is On, F8 only works for Lexia. F6 and F7 are Lexia's only while it is
    speaking or paused; the rest of the time they work in other programs and games.
    Turn Lexia Off to get F8 back, or pick other keys on the settings page.
  - Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" the first time. This is because Lexia
    is a new program that is not digitally signed yet. Click "More info", then
    "Run anyway".
  - Lexia reads English only for now, and its own words are in English. More languages
    will come later.
  - If Windows reports a missing file such as VCRUNTIME140.dll, install the
    "Microsoft Visual C++ Redistributable (x64)" from microsoft.com and try again.

UNINSTALL
  Quit Lexia from the tray icon and delete the folder. If you turned on run at startup,
  turn it off first. Your settings and voices are in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Lexia; delete that
  folder too if you want them gone.
