The line icons of the Settings window and the Control bar.
Stratis UI Icons (CC BY 4.0), https://www.figma.com/community/file/1177180791780461401/stratis-ui-icons-1000-free-figma-icons
except discord.svg, github.svg and itch.svg (the logos of those sites). See licenses/components/ui-icons.txt.

The SVG files have a fixed colour (#6E504A). The native Settings window and the Control bar recolour them in C++ (svg_recolour.h) before Direct2D draws them.

The tray icons src/res/lexia.ico and lexia_off.ico are not made from these files: they come from the Lexia logo
SVGs src/res/lexia_logo.svg and lexia_logo_off.svg (the Off one draws volume-07 of this set inside the logo; see
licenses/components/ui-engravings.txt and scripts/make_tray_icons.ps1).

Changed from the original: user-profile-03.svg draws its two outlines as stroked ellipses instead of a 4 wide stroke
inside an SVG <mask> (Direct2D's SVG renderer, used by the native Settings window, does not support masks); the picture
is the same.
