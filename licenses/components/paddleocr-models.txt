Used for: finding and reading the text in a Shot (the folder models\: det.onnx,
rec_en.onnx, rec_cyrillic.onnx, dict_en.txt, dict_cyrillic.txt).
  - det.onnx is ch_PP-OCRv4_det, from https://huggingface.co/SWHL/RapidOCR (Apache-2.0),
    an ONNX conversion not changed otherwise.
  - rec_en.onnx is MODIFIED: PaddleOCR's en_PP-OCRv3_rec, fine-tuned by the Lexia project
    from PaddleOCR's published training checkpoint on synthetic lines in pixel game fonts and
    normal fonts, so that it reads game text. Same architecture, input and output as the
    original. The training scripts are in training/rec_en of
    https://github.com/Xencaye/Lexia; the notice is also in the model's metadata. The fonts
    the training lines were drawn in are listed under "Fonts used to train rec_en.onnx".
  - dict_en.txt is en_PP-OCRv3_rec's dictionary and rec_cyrillic.onnx / dict_cyrillic.txt are
    cyrillic_PP-OCRv3_rec, from https://huggingface.co/deepghs/paddleocr, not changed
    otherwise. That repository is under the "Model Distribution Disclaimer License" (RDL),
    which says the original licence of each model applies: PaddleOCR's Apache-2.0.
