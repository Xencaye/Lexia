Used for: finding and reading the text in a Shot (the folder models\: det.onnx,
rec_en.onnx, rec_cyrillic.onnx, dict_en.txt, dict_cyrillic.txt).
The files are ONNX conversions of PaddleOCR's models, not changed otherwise:
  - det.onnx is ch_PP-OCRv4_det, from https://huggingface.co/SWHL/RapidOCR (Apache-2.0).
  - rec_en.onnx / dict_en.txt are en_PP-OCRv3_rec and rec_cyrillic.onnx / dict_cyrillic.txt are
    cyrillic_PP-OCRv3_rec, from https://huggingface.co/deepghs/paddleocr. That repository is
    under the "Model Distribution Disclaimer License" (RDL), which says the original licence of
    each model applies: PaddleOCR's Apache-2.0.
