Fonts used to train rec_en.onnx (credit only: no font is shipped)
Used for: drawing the synthetic training lines that Lexia's English recogniser (rec_en.onnx,
see the PaddleOCR entry) was fine-tuned on. The fonts are not part of Lexia and are not in the
model; this list credits their authors. Each font was used under its own licence, checked in
its download: SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL), CC0 1.0, BSD-2-Clause, Apache-2.0 or the Ubuntu
Font Licence 1.0 (UFL). Sources and hashes: training/rec_en/sources.json and
docs/notes/training-fonts.md in https://github.com/Xencaye/Lexia.
