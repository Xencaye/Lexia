Trained by Bryce Beattie (https://brycebeattie.com/files/tts/) with Piper, from scratch, for
1000 epochs, on the LJ Speech Dataset by Keith Ito (https://keithito.com/LJ-Speech-Dataset/).
The LJ Speech Dataset is in the public domain: its texts are from public domain books and its
audio was recorded for LibriVox, whose recordings are public domain. Its author says there are
no restrictions on its use. The model card in rhasspy/piper-voices gives the dataset licence as
"public domain" and states no separate licence for the model weights; Beattie's page lists the
Voice as "License: public domain". No attribution is required. Credit is given as a courtesy.
