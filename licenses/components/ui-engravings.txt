Source: Old Book Illustrations, https://www.oldbookillustrations.com/ (scans of illustrations from old books)
Used for: the pictures of the Settings window (src\res\img\: bee.svg, dog.svg, flute.svg, otter.svg, wine.svg,
wolf.svg, vectorised from the scans by the Lexia project; tinted by the window) and the Lexia logo (src\res\lexia_logo.svg, and lexia_logo_off.svg for the Off
look, from which scripts\make_tray_icons.ps1 makes the exe and tray icons src\res\lexia.ico and lexia_off.ico).
The engraving in the logo is "Faust—Scene III Headpiece", https://www.oldbookillustrations.com/illustrations/headpiece-scene-3/ ;
the logo itself (the rounded square, the crop, the Off version) is the Lexia project's own design made from it.
The Off logo also contains the Stratis UI icon volume-07 (CC BY 4.0, see ui-icons.txt).
