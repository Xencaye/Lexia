Spec for the Win32 bar

The bar is drawn by Lexia in plain Win32 (GDI+ into a layered window, the icons by Direct2D). Sizes are in px at 100 % DPI (96 dpi); scale them with the monitor's DPI. The numbers are measured from the owner's wireframe/control_bar_large.png (433 x 60) and control_bar_small.png (250 x 60), and are in src/control_bar_layout.cpp, which is tested. The words come from controlBar.*.

Position and size

  • Top centre of the Overlay by default, 24 px from the top edge. The user drags it; the position and the size (Large or Small) are remembered. When a size change makes the bar wider or narrower, keep its centre.
  • Drag by the grip (the dots) or by any empty part of the bar. Keep the bar fully inside the monitor.
  • The bar exists only during a Session. There is no Close button: the user leaves the Overlay with a right-click, or with the Read key.

The Analysis has no status well: while it runs, Pause, Skip and Stop are greyed and disabled.

Clicks and drags on the bar are never Picks

  • The bar is its own window above the Overlay. A click or a drag that starts on it never reaches the Pick code.
  • A drag that starts on the bar moves the bar. A drag that starts on the Overlay and passes over the bar stays a Drag of the Overlay.

Nothing needs focus (ADR-0001)

  • No text input, no control that needs the keyboard. Every button works with the mouse only; the keys stay the registered hotkeys.
  • The window is WS_EX_NOACTIVATE | WS_EX_TOPMOST | WS_EX_TOOLWINDOW; WM_MOUSEACTIVATE answers MA_NOACTIVATE; no SetForegroundWindow or SetFocus.
  • Help ends the Session as a right-click does and only then opens the Settings window on its Help page.

Colours

Light (the design): paper #F5EFE8, tile #BEA991, ink #6D6154, line #D9CFC4, accent #6E504A. Dark is a temporary palette of the same roles (TODO(owner): final dark palette): paper #2A2420, tile #8F7A6B, word #E8DED3, line #574C44, accent #C9A79E. Light and Dark follow the Theme setting.
