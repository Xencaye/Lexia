Spec for D2

    • A native Win32 popup menu (CreatePopupMenu, TrackPopupMenu). Windows draws it, in the user's own light or dark mode and with its own font: Lexia does not paint it and the neumorphic style does not apply. Call SetForegroundWindow on Lexia's hidden window before TrackPopupMenu and post WM_NULL after it, as Windows asks for tray menus. The Overlay is not up when a tray menu is open (the tray is used outside a Session), so ADR-0001 is not in play.
    • Rows, in this order: On and Off as a pair of radio items (the current one has the dot: MFT_RADIOCHECK and MFS_CHECKED), a separator, Open Lexia (the default item, bold, also the double-click on the icon), Quit. The words are tray.on, tray.off, tray.open, tray.quit. No keys are shown: there is no menu accelerator.
    • On and Off call App::SetOn; Open Lexia opens the Settings window (it takes focus: it is an ordinary window); Quit calls App::Quit().
    • The tray icon is 16 px (and the 20, 24, 32 px sizes for high DPI). The tooltip of the icon says On or Off. Off needs a second look that does not depend on colour: here the same icon, grey, with a slash.
